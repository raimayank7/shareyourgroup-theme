Comments are for questions, tips and feedback about ShareYourGroup and our articles. Keep them helpful and kind. By commenting you agree to these rules.

✅ Welcome

  • Questions about a post, a category or how the site works
  • Feedback and ideas to make ShareYourGroup better
  • Tips that help others find good groups
  • Reporting a broken or spammy listing (politely)
  • Friendly, respectful replies to other people

🚫 Not allowed

  • Group links in comments. Use the Submit page instead; it's faster and your group gets its own card.
  • Spam, ads and self-promotion
  • Abuse, hate speech, threats or harassment
  • Adult content, scams, “earn money” offers or phone numbers
  • Fake or misleading information
  • Repeated off-topic comments

How we moderate

  • Comments run on Disqus. Disqus may hold some comments for review automatically.
  • We remove comments that break these rules, usually without notice.
  • People who keep breaking the rules may be blocked from commenting.

Your privacy

Never post your phone number, email, address or any OTP in a comment; anyone can read it. See our Privacy Policy for how comments are handled.

Report a comment

Use the flag on the comment in Disqus, or contact us with a link to it.
