Get more members by listing your WhatsApp group or channel on ShareYourGroup. First copy your invite link, then drop it here. It's free and takes under a minute.

1. Copy your invite link

🤖

Android

  1. Open the group
  2. Tap the group name at the top
  3. Tap Invite via link
  4. Tap Copy link
🍎

iPhone

  1. Open the group
  2. Tap the group name
  3. Tap Invite to Group via Link
  4. Tap Copy Link
💻

WhatsApp Web / Desktop

  1. Open the group
  2. Click the group name
  3. Click Invite to group via link
  4. Click Copy link

Only admins see the invite option unless the admin allows everyone. The link looks like https://chat.whatsapp.com/…

Channel? Open it, tap the channel name, then Share → copy the link (https://whatsapp.com/channel/…).

2. Drop it on ShareYourGroup

  1. Paste the link into the box at the top of the homepage or on the Submit page.
  2. Agree to the sharing rules (once per visit).
  3. Pick a category and write one line about who the group is for.
  4. Tap Publish. We fetch the name and photo from WhatsApp for you.

3. Grow faster

🖼️

Look trustworthy

A clear name, a real group photo and a description that says what members get.

📣

Share your listing

Tap the share button on your card and post it on Instagram, Telegram or your WhatsApp status.

📌

Pin your rules

Language, no spam, no forwards. Good rules keep members from leaving.

🔁

Bump it

Submit the same link again to move back to the top, once every 24 hours.

Removing or changing a listing

The name and photo update from WhatsApp automatically. To remove your group, reset the invite link in WhatsApp: the old listing stops working and is removed. Then submit the new link if you want to stay listed.

Groups that break the sharing rules (adult, scams, selling numbers, hate) are removed. Spamming other groups with your link can get your WhatsApp number banned.
