Joining a group from ShareYourGroup takes about 10 seconds. Here's how it works, what to check before you join, and what to do if a link doesn't work.

Join a group in 4 steps

  1. Find a group. Use the search box on the homepage, tap a category chip (Jobs, Study, Gaming…) or switch between Groups and Channels.
  2. Check the card. Read the name, category and description. A CHANNEL tag means it's a one-way channel, not a chat group.
  3. Tap “Join group”. Read the short safety tips, tick I understand and tap Continue to WhatsApp.
  4. Tap “Join group” in WhatsApp (or Follow for a channel). Some groups ask the admin to approve you first.
📱

On a phone

The WhatsApp app opens straight away. If it doesn't, make sure WhatsApp is installed and updated.

💻

On a computer

WhatsApp Web or the desktop app opens. Link your phone first at web.whatsapp.com.

“This invite link was reset” or “Couldn't join”?

  • The link was reset. The admin made a new link and the old one stopped working.
  • The group is full. WhatsApp groups hold up to 1,024 members.
  • Admin approval is on. Your request is waiting for the admin.

Tap the 🚩 on the card and choose Link not working. We re-check links and remove dead ones automatically.

Stay safe

🔐

Never share codes

OTPs, passwords, UPI PIN or bank details. No real admin, bank or company asks for them.

💸

Don't pay to join

“VIP” entry fees, task jobs and money-doubling offers are scams.

🔗

Skip unknown apps

Don't install APK files or open strange links posted in groups.

Lost money to a scam in India? Call the cyber-crime helpline 1930 or report it at cybercrime.gov.in.
