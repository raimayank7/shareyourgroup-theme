This policy explains what information ShareYourGroup (“we”, “us”, www.shareyourgroup.com) collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain.

What we collect · Cookies & ads · How we use it · Who we share with · How long we keep it · Your choices · Contact

1. What we collect

When you submit a group or channel

  • The invite link, the category and the description you type.
  • The group's public name and photo, which we read from WhatsApp's public invite page.
  • The date and time of the submission.

We don't ask for your name, phone number or email to submit a group. Submitted listings are public: anyone can see and join them.

When you report a listing

The link you reported and the reason you chose. Reports are anonymous.

When you use the contact form

Your name, email address and message. Blogger (Google) delivers the message to us by email.

When you comment

Comments on posts and pages are run by Disqus. Disqus collects the information you give it under its own privacy policy.

Automatically

  • Usage data through Google Analytics: pages viewed, device and browser type, rough location (city level), how you arrived, and actions such as searches, joins and shares. We don't see your name or phone number.
  • Settings saved in your browser (local storage): light or dark mode, auto-scroll on or off, whether you hid a notice or the join tips, and links you've reported, so we don't ask twice. This stays on your device.

2. Cookies and advertising

We show ads through Google AdSense to keep the site free.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners show ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can turn off personalised ads in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors at aboutads.info.
  • Learn more: How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see a consent message before personalised ads or analytics cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy link in that message.

Some content is marked sponsored (for example, partner banners). If you sign up through those links, we may earn a commission. The partner then handles your information under its own policy.

3. How we use information

  • To show group listings, search and categories.
  • To check that links still work and to remove dead, reported or rule-breaking listings.
  • To reply to messages you send us.
  • To understand which features help people and to fix problems.
  • To show ads that pay for the site.

We don't sell your personal information.

4. Who we share it with

  • Google: Blogger (hosting), Google Sheets and Apps Script (stores submitted listings), Google Analytics, and Google AdSense.
  • GitHub hosts the site's design files and a public copy of the group list.
  • Disqus runs comments.
  • WhatsApp: when you tap Continue to WhatsApp, you leave our site and WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Listings stay until the invite link stops working, the listing breaks our rules, or you ask us to remove it.
  • Contact messages are kept for as long as we need them to help you.
  • Analytics data is kept for Google Analytics' standard retention period.

6. Your choices

  • Remove a listing: reset the group's invite link in WhatsApp, or contact us with the link.
  • Ads: use the Google Ads Settings link above.
  • Browser settings: clearing your browser's site data removes the settings we save on your device.
  • Access or delete anything else you've sent us: contact us and we'll help.

7. Children

ShareYourGroup is not meant for children under 13, and WhatsApp itself requires users to be at least 13 (older in some countries). We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Links to other sites

Listings link to WhatsApp, and some pages link to other sites such as our tools on GroupChatOn. We're not responsible for how those sites handle your information.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Use our contact page.
