Send a WhatsApp message to any number without saving it to your contacts. Pick the country, type the number and your message, then tap Open in WhatsApp.

How it works

  1. Choose the country code, for example +91 for India.
  2. Type the phone number without the leading 0 or country code.
  3. Write a message (optional) and tap Open in WhatsApp. The chat opens with your message ready to send.
