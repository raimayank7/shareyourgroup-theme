List your WhatsApp group or channel for free. Paste the invite link, pick a category and add one line about it. No account needed.

Get your invite link in 3 taps

  1. Open your group in WhatsApp and tap the group name at the top.
  2. Tap “Invite via link”. Only admins usually see this option.
  3. Tap “Copy link” and paste it in the box above. It looks like https://chat.whatsapp.com/…

For a channel: open it, tap the channel name, then Share and copy the link (https://whatsapp.com/channel/…). Step-by-step pictures: How to share your group.

What happens next

⚡

Live straight away

Your card appears at the top of the list. If WhatsApp is slow to share the name and photo, it shows up within about 15 minutes.

🔁

Bump once a day

Submit the same link again to move your group back to the top, once every 24 hours.

🧹

Kept fresh

We re-check links regularly. Dead or reset links are removed automatically.

Sharing rules

✅ Share

  • An active invite link that works
  • A clear name and an honest description
  • The category that really fits

🚫 Never share

  • Adult / 18+ or “paid friendship” groups
  • “Earn money”, money-doubling or other scams
  • Selling phone numbers, OTPs or accounts
  • Hate, violence, drugs or fake news

Listings that break these rules are removed. See the Disclaimer and Privacy Policy.
